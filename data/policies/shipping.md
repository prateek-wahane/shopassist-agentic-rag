# Shipping policy

## Standard shipping
Standard shipping costs $5.99 and takes 3-5 business days.

## Free shipping
Orders over $75 ship free with standard shipping. Exception: bulky items such as treadmills (EQ- series equipment over 20 kg) are never eligible for free shipping and carry a flat $49 delivery fee.

## Express shipping
Express shipping costs $14.99 and arrives in 1-2 business days if ordered before 2 pm.

## Delivery area
We currently ship only within the United States.
