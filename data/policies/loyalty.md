# Loyalty programme

## Earning points
Members earn 1 point for every $1 spent. Points are not earned on gift card purchases.

## Redeeming points
100 points can be redeemed for $5 off an order. Points cannot be combined with other promotions.

## Discount codes
StrideStore never issues discount or coupon codes through chat. Promotions are announced only by email to subscribed members.
