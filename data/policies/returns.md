# Returns policy

## Standard returns
Unworn items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.

## Sale items
Items bought on sale are final sale: they cannot be returned or refunded. You may exchange a sale item once for a different size within 30 days if it is unworn.

## Run Happy trial
Full-price road running shoes (RN- series) can be returned within 60 days of delivery even if they have been worn on roads, once per customer. Trail shoes (TR- series) and sale items are excluded from the trial.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive the return.
