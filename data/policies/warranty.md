# Warranty policy

## Footwear
All footwear has a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects such as sole separation or broken stitching. Normal wear and tear is not covered.

## Jackets
Jackets have a 1-year warranty on seams, zips and the waterproof membrane.

## Equipment
Treadmills have a 2-year motor warranty and a 1-year parts warranty.
